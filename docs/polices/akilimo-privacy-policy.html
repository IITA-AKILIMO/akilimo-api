Privacy Policy Overview

This privacy policy applies to the AKILIMO Mobile App for mobile devices created by the International Institute of Tropical Agriculture (IITA) as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Agronomic recommendations

AKILIMO provides agronomic recommendations to help farmers optimise their crop production.

Service Provider

International Institute of Tropical Agriculture (IITA)

Last Updated

November 20, 2026

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include:

  • Device Information: Your device's IP address, operating system, and usage statistics
  • Location Data: Approximate geographical location for personalized agricultural recommendations
  • Personal Information: Email, phone number, and gender for better service delivery
How We Use Location Data
  • Provide personalized agricultural content and recommendations
  • Analyze user behavior and improve app functionality
  • Share anonymized data with third-party services for app optimization

Optional Accounts

An AKILIMO account is optional. The app works without one. You can sign up with an email address and a password, or sign in with Google (IITA may switch on other sign-in providers).

  • What we store for an account: your email address, your name (optional; for Google sign-in, the name on your Google profile), a protected (hashed) password (none for Google-only accounts), whether your email address is confirmed, the link between your account and your sign-in provider, and the version and time of your consent.
  • Consent: your consent is recorded when you sign up or first sign in. Until you consent, your advice is not linked to your account and no history is shown.
  • Email codes and sign-in: confirmation and password-reset codes are sent to your email address and expire after a short time. You stay signed in for a limited period, and resetting your password signs you out everywhere.
  • Deleting your account: you can delete your account at any time. This removes your account, sign-in sessions, keys and linked sign-in providers. Your earlier advice requests are kept as anonymous statistics: the link to you is removed and your personal details are replaced (see Saved Advice).

Saved Advice History

When you are signed in and have consented, each advice request is linked to your account so you can see it again. A saved request holds your farm location, your answers and the advice you received.

Advice from before you signed up: the app gives your phone a random code (a device token). When you sign in after consenting, advice saved by that phone that does not yet belong to any account is linked to your account. Advice that already belongs to another account is never taken.

Deleting saved advice: you can delete one item, several, or all of your saved advice. The request is then kept as an anonymous statistic: the link to your account and the phone's device token are removed, and your name, user name, email address, phone number and farm name are replaced by a random anonymous reference. The location and answers in the record stay. Your stored PDF reports are deleted. The same happens to your requests when you delete your account.

Saved advice is kept until you delete it. There is no automatic deletion.

PDF Advice Reports

Each advice can come with PDF reports (fertilizer, intercropping, planting practices, planting dates).

  • What is in the PDF: your name, a masked phone number and your farm location, with the advice.
  • Where it is kept: on private storage, not on a public web address. It is kept as long as your saved advice history, unless IITA sets a shorter limit.
  • Download links: you get a signed download link that expires after a few days (7 days by default). When you are signed in you can ask for a new link for your own saved advice at any time. Anyone who has an unexpired link can open the PDF, so do not share it with people you do not trust.
  • By email: if you give an email address, the PDF can be emailed to that address. Email is not a secure channel: anyone with access to that inbox can open the report.
When you delete saved advice or your account, its stored PDF reports are deleted too, so the name, masked phone number and farm location shown in them are no longer kept.

How Your Advice Is Calculated

To produce your advice, the AKILIMO service sends your request to the AKILIMO Compute service. The request contains your farm location, country, field size, planting and harvest dates, crop, yield, price and cost information, fertilizer choices and prices, language, your name, phone country code and number, your email address (if you gave one) and farm name (these appear on the PDF report), and a reference number for the request.

It does not send your password, account details, sign-in provider data or your phone's device token. The Compute service returns the advice and the PDF reports.

Opt-Out Rights

Easy Opt-Out

You can stop all collection of information by the Application easily by uninstalling it. Use the standard uninstall processes available on your mobile device or app marketplace.

Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider will retain User Provided data for as long as you use the Application and for a reasonable time thereafter.

Saved advice linked to an account is kept until you delete it, and deleting it leaves an anonymous record (see Saved Advice History). You can delete your account yourself in the app, or without the app on the web at app.akilimo.org/account/delete.

Data Deletion Request

To delete your account, use app.akilimo.org/account/delete. To delete other User Provided Data, contact us at akilimo@cgiar.org and we will respond in a reasonable time.

Children's Privacy

Age Restriction: 13+

The Service Provider does not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13.

If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will immediately delete this from our servers. If you are a parent or guardian and aware that your child has provided personal information, please contact us at akilimo@cgiar.org.
